Top-billed cast

        How to play

        1. A random movie is picked and its top 10 billed actors are hidden.
        2. The first actor is revealed. Guess the movie!
        3. You start with 1000 points and get 10 guesses.
        4. Each wrong guess costs 100 points and reveals the next actor.
        5. Hints cost 50 points each: genre, release year, director, or the title's letter pattern (e.g. *** **********).
        6. Win or lose, the poster is revealed at the end.
